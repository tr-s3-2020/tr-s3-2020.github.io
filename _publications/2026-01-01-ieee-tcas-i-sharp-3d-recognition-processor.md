First-author journal paper. A 28nm digital neuromorphic SoC for ultra-low-power edge-side 3D point-cloud perception (LiDAR-based autonomous driving and embodied-intelligence sensing), reaching 0.13 mJ/frame energy consumption and 1.01 ms latency — a world-class result in edge-side 3D point-cloud perception systems. TCAS-I is the flagship journal of the IEEE Circuits and Systems Society.

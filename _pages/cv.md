Research projects
======
* **28nm digital neuromorphic chip for 3D point-cloud perception** — project lead, 2024.12 – 2025.12
  * Designed a 28nm digital neuromorphic processor, completed tape-out and testing.
  * Application: ultra-low-power edge-side 3D point-cloud perception (LiDAR-based autonomous driving / embodied intelligence).
  * Chip achieved 0.13 mJ/frame energy and 1.01 ms latency, at world-leading level for edge-side 3D point-cloud perception.
  * Result published in *IEEE TCAS-I* as first author.
* **Spiking neural network for polar sea-ice remote-sensing segmentation** — algorithm team lead, 2024.09 – 2024.12
  * Designed an SNN algorithm and tested it together with a novel neuromorphic vision sensor.
  * Application: sea-ice remote-sensing image segmentation in oceanographic polar expeditions.
  * Demonstrated the high dynamic-range response of a transistor-memristor hybrid neuromorphic vision sensor from an algorithmic application perspective.
  * Result published in *Nature Communications* as second author.
* **28nm digital neuromorphic chip for embodied intelligence** — project lead, 2023.09 – 2024.09
  * Designed a 28nm digital neuromorphic processor, completed tape-out and testing.
  * Application: dedicated accelerator for edge-side embodied-intelligence systems.
  * Chip reached 57.7 TOPS/W energy efficiency; the first dedicated neuromorphic accelerator for embodied intelligence.
  * Result published in *IEEE TCAS-AI* as co-first author.
* **FDUROP Xiyuan Project: disease-expression detection system**, Fudan University, 2020.06 – 2021.02
  * Project lead; designed a YOLO-based specific-disease expression detection system deployed on an NVIDIA Jetson development board.
  * Funded by Fudan's FDUROP and National Key Laboratory of Integrated Chips and Systems; successfully completed.
